Model credits

Hostile Takeover combines its own geometry and textures with adapted models by Gerardo Justel (re1monsen). The source models and texture maps are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Sterna migrant fighters

Hostile Takeover created the twin pressure hulls, bridge designs, radiator cassettes, drives, markings and material maps. The circulation pumps and a sample of coating wear were adapted from Justel’s Sci fi Portable Generator.

Modular ships

The axial assemblies combine original castings, panelwork and sockets with cut, rescaled and reoriented donor geometry and surface maps. Individual source models and adaptations are listed in the ship sections, workshop sources and faction kits.

Orbital bases and megastructures

Hostile Takeover reassembled licensed station sectors and machines into bearing frames, role-specific machinery and service modules, retained the source UVs and materials, and added position lights. The missile cassettes use original Hostile Takeover geometry. The turret combines the artist’s cannon maps with local graphite and oxide finishes. The station source list and industrial platform source list identify the models and link to their artist listings.
