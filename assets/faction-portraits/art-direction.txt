Hostile Takeover — Faction portrait artwork

Original alien anatomy, industrial pressure garments and orbital interiors generated for this project with OpenAI ImageGen. The game recombines independent heads, compatible sensory organs, garments and rooms locally from the world seed and faction seat. Each source atlas contains six specimens, read left to right and top to bottom. The shipped WebP files were cropped, keyed by their original alpha, framed and compressed from these source atlases.

The convoy series adds four individual crew heads, six outfits and six inhabited ship interiors. Full source prompts, checksums and preparation instructions are in nomad-studies.txt alongside this file.

Source plate index
Head files 01–06 are crown, 07–12 delta, 13–18 mantle, 19–24 pelagic and 25–30 tide. Garments 01–06 are workwear and 07–12 workwear-b. Rooms 01–06 are rooms and 07–12 rooms-b. Sensory organs 01–06 are organs. Each head has two hand-fitted optional organ types. Files ending in -128 or -64 are the small-screen versions.

Head files 31–35 are individual high-resolution dossier studies. Their prompts, processing notes and source digests are in dossier-studies.txt in this directory.

organs
Create an original transparent RGBA atlas of SIX different biological lateral head appendages for Hostile Takeover, a serious industrial science fiction faction portrait engine. Imagine a xenobiology studio photographed six isolated small practical creature props, each a single optional alien ear / sensory crest. This is production art to composite behind previously painted alien heads. No heads, no busts, no full bodies. ONLY SIX SEPARATE INDIVIDUAL APPENDAGES, one single organ in each cell, NOT PAIRS. Clean transparent interior and exterior negative space. No backdrop or ground contact shadows. 1536x1024 landscape canvas divided into a strict invisible grid of THREE equal columns by TWO equal rows, six distinct square cells. Leave a generous transparent gap at every gridline. Respect 8 percent margin inside each cell. No frames, text, letters or UI, no overlapping cells. In EVERY cell orient the same way: the thick biological attachment base of the organ starts near the LOWER LEFT corner of the individual square and the tip extends diagonally outward toward the UPPER RIGHT. The appendage will root at the temple of a creature hidden behind its cheekplate. Show the whole isolated single organ in three-quarter frontal view. Grounded, museum quality natural anatomy, subtly worn bone and cartilage, detailed subsurface amber and veined membranes, clear silhouette against nothing. Restrained mostly warm limestone, copper, graphite and amber material family. Warm upper-left key with a gentle blue rim on shaded edge, practical physical texture. Do not use gore, blood, tobacco, clothing, hardware or metal.
