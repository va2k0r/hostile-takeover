Hostile Takeover — Synthetic faction portrait artwork

Seven original artificial anatomies made for Hostile Takeover with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool using transparent_background=true. Each was generated separately as a 1254×1254 RGBA image. The two WebP resolutions keep the native alpha and are resized in premultiplied alpha. Full PNGs and their framing recipe are archived under output/assets-source/synthetic-portraits.
