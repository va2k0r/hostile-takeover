Gerardo Justel (re1monsen) created the six original models and their textures. He released them under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Artist portfolio · License

Hostile Takeover separated the mechanical assemblies, aligned their mounting faces, built two levels of detail and optimized the original surface maps for the game's lighting.

Scout Drone Sketch

The sensor pod comes from one side of the optical housing. A section of its armored shell forms the scanning dome. Original model

Drone Concept

The drone's long cylindrical body, upper drive assembly and lower air intake supply the reactor, engine bell and vent cowl. Original model

Rigged Sci-Fi Lift – Mobile Platform – Elevator

The platform's rim and spokes form an open dock. Its linkage and bearing supply a lifting boom and a clamp hub. Original model

Sci fi Portable Generator

The paired pump lines, side connector, coolant elbows and reinforced housing form the processing and freight fittings. Original model

Spaceship 4

Two centerline hull panels form the keel. A single outer armor bank forms the swept blade. Original model

Spaceship 5

The side rails, upper housing and exposed frame provide the split vane, bridge shell and gantry. Original model
