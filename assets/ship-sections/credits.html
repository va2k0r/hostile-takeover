Source hulls

  • Christmas 2022 – The Derelict. Its armored shell supplies all three Derelict capital sections and further options for each ship role. Original model
  • UFO Doodle. The Saucer crescent, belt and quarter preserve the radial plates of the disc. Its plates also appear on the visor, transom and cross-shaped sections. Original model
  • Drone. The flying wing with twin ducts supplies the Wing sweep, Lift fans and more aerodynamic sections. Original model
  • Drone Sketch. Sections of its spherical hull form the Survey lens, Orbital cab, Orbital drive and Spherical work chamber. Original model
  • Space Station. The Dock launch bow and Dock courier cab come from the forward hulls of two separate small vessels berthed at the station. The source objects are spacestation.026 and spacestation.027. Original model
  • Procedural Spaceship. Cut into patrol foredecks, bodywork and stern structures, including the Patrol afterburner. Original model
  • Spaceship 4. Its armored hull provides the Dreadnought foredeck, spine and drives. Original model
  • Spaceship 5. Its chassis supplies the Hauler cab, chassis and transom. Original model
  • 2.3K Followers Celebration – Spaceship. The A01 sections use its original hull. Its surface finish also contributes to the workshop’s painted pressure plating. Original model

Workshop adaptations

Hostile Takeover cut the original triangles on transverse planes, retained their UV coordinates, optimized geometry and surface maps, and added closed pressure transitions, paint, lighted exhausts and structural fittings. The direct-cut family uses a single uniform scale for the original fragment. Its adapter occupies a short span outside the two cut planes. The shaped families use separate pressure hulls and plated branches, wells, crossbeams, forks or stacked drives.
