Interactive score

Music by Kevin MacLeod, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Decisions, Spacial Winds, Long Note Four, Vanes, The Voices, Unseen Horrors, Controlled Chaos, Tenebrous Brothers Carnival - Act Three, Stormfront, The Escalation, Rising, Industrial Cinematic, It is Lost.

Decisions plays in full. Hostile Takeover uses edited excerpts, level adjustments, fades and loops for the other tracks.

Fonderia

The English command voice uses the first Fonderia audition. The remaining lines use the same blend of Emma, Lily and Isabella from Kokoro v1.0, with original pitch contours and steel resonances from Il ferro di Orison. The game plays local recordings.

3D assets

Original models by Gerardo Justel (re1monsen), acquired free of charge on Fab on November 16, 17 and 20, 2026, and used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Derived geometry and textures retain attribution to the artist. Adaptations are listed below. Models 03 (Artemis) and 23 (Power Cell) were not purchased or included in the game.

  • Procedural Spaceship

    Fighter hull with two mesh detail levels. Close inspections load the native colour texture at up to 4096 px. Original normals, panel wear and UVs are retained.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • Spaceship 4

    Hull for the laser corvette. Two mesh detail levels, original colour textures up to 4096 px on inspection and 2048 px normal maps. Military navigation lights and laser sockets follow the hull.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • Spaceship 5

    Local and FTL freighters share this hull and its two mesh detail levels. The local ship carries four containers. The larger FTL ship carries one longitudinal container on each side. Both use containers 14 or 27, original colour textures up to 4096 px on inspection and a 2048 px normal map.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • Space Station Modules

    The gantry, platform and machinery are available as individual components in the Hangar.

    The complete H-shaped station is available as an individual model in the Hangar.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • Spacestation 7 - Procedural

    Each mine tower is a vertical slab cut through the shoulders and complete tapered shaft of model 13. The C-shaped station itself is also in the Hangar. The asteroid, debris and display shuttle were excluded.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • Space Station 3

    Sub-FTL factions use the complete double-ring orbital base by Gerardo Justel (re1monsen). Both decks, radial fittings and original PBR textures are retained. The display particles were removed and the base was optimized for two levels of detail.

    A standalone 34° cut of the upper annulus is available in the Hangar. Original plating and fittings retain their UVs. The exposed cut faces are capped and UV-mapped.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • Space Station 4

    Each of the shipyard’s three dry docks combines a section from each original annulus. The upper deck stops short of the lower apron and the original axial spine joins both decks at the back. Each refinery unit uses two unequal wheel sectors mounted upright as saddles. The axial shaft forms the two columns and the horizontal condenser. The column gallery and pipe elbows are also cut from the original wheel. The standalone 24° upper-deck study is in the Hangar. Cut surfaces were closed and UV-mapped.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • 100K Views - SciFi Crate Container

    Model 14 is a container. Only the display surface was removed. All 19 container meshes retain their proportions, UVs and materials. Two mesh detail levels, 2048 px normal maps and a 4096 px colour map for close inspection. Resource paint is confined to panel accents.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • Scifi - Crate - Container

    Model 27 uses the high-resolution orange variant with its original gauge. Display copies were removed. The container was oriented horizontally and scaled uniformly. Two mesh detail levels, 2048 px normal maps and a 4096 px colour map for close inspection. Resource paint is confined to panel accents.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • 2.3K Followers Celebration - Spaceship

    Builder hull. The game preserves the original shape and surface textures and uses two mesh detail levels.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

  • Black Honey - Robotic Arm

    The builder carries the original robotic arm and claw on its starboard foredeck. The game uses the working arm from the display scene, scaled to the ship and optimized for two levels of detail.

    Fab source · Original on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

Asteroid belts

Five X-ray microtomography scans of the NWA 869 meteorite by David Čapek, Astronomical Institute of the Czech Academy of Sciences (2025). Samples L01D192a, L01D166a, L01D180a, L01D057a and L01D109a were simplified, centered and scaled for real-time rendering.

Orbital platforms

The Autonomous Turret, Shield Generator, Fissile Extractor and Crystal Harvester combine sections of eight models by Gerardo Justel (re1monsen), licensed under CC BY 4.0. The assemblies retain the artist’s UVs and textures, with new bearings, emitter rings, separator bands and collection equipment by Hostile Takeover. Source models, original listings and adaptations.

The artillery missile uses adapted propulsion and collar sections from Cylinoid 005 by Gerardo Justel (re1monsen), licensed under CC BY 4.0. The casing, fins and nozzle were designed for Hostile Takeover. Asset provenance
